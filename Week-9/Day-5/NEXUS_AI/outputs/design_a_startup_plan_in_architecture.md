# Final Answer (Reporter Output)

## Startup Plan – “ArcStudio” (Architecture Design Studio)  

**Goal:** Build a lean, technology‑enabled architectural practice that wins $5 M of design contracts in the first 12‑18 months, reaches break‑even by month 14, and scales to a $2‑5 M ARR SaaS‑plus‑services business within three years.

---  

### 1. Executive Summary  
- **Mission:** Deliver high‑impact, sustainable building designs faster and cheaper through a hybrid model of boutique consultancy + a subscription‑based BIM‑as‑a‑Service (BaaS) platform.  
- **Target Market:** Mid‑size commercial developers and corporate real‑estate teams in North America and Europe that need 10‑50 k sq ft projects (office fit‑outs, mixed‑use, boutique hotels).  
- **Unique Value:** 1) Rapid concept‑to‑construction‑drawings (≤ 8 weeks) via a proprietary cloud‑BIM workflow, 2) Fixed‑price, outcome‑based fees (percentage‑of‑construction cost + monthly SaaS), 3) Integrated sustainability analytics that cut energy‑use forecasts by 10‑15 %.  
- **Financial Target:** $5 M revenue in Year 1 (≈ 50 projects @ $100k each + $2 M SaaS ARR), break‑even month 14, cash‑burn ≤ $250k / yr.

| **Economic downturn in construction** | Reduced project pipeline | Medium‑High | Diversify into renovation & adaptive‑reuse markets, increase SaaS focus (recurring revenue) |
